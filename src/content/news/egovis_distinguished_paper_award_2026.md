Our papers **"Differentiable Task Graph Learning"** (NeurIPS 2024) and **"Ego-Exo4D"** (CVPR 2024) have received the **EgoVis Distinguished Paper Award 2024/2025**, announced at the EgoVis Workshop at CVPR 2026. Big congrats to [Luigi Seminara](team/luigi_seminara) and all co-authors!
