Our paper [**"ViterbiPlanNet: Injecting Procedural Knowledge via Differentiable Viterbi for Planning in Instructional Videos"**](research/seminara2026viterbiplannet) has been selected for the **Efficient CVPR Badge** as part of CVPR 2026's inaugural Compute Reporting Initiative.

This recognition highlights methods that demonstrate outstanding computational efficiency together with transparent compute reporting. Big congrats to [Luigi Seminara](team/luigi_seminara) and all coauthors for this achievement.
