---
title: "Mission & Vision"
subtitle: "Our research philosophy and vision for the future of human-AI collaboration."
whoWeAre: "The Collaborative AI (Co-AI) group is part of the Image Processing Laboratory (IPLab) at the University of Catania. We study how AI systems can perceive and understand the real world from an embodied, first-person perspective, enabling timely, context-aware feedback and assistance."
visionMain: "We envision AI assistants that perceive the world through wearable and ambient sensors, build rich episodic memories of what has happened, and reason about what should happen next to help people achieve their goals. Rather than replacing human action, these systems are designed to support it by anticipating future steps, identifying mistakes, and providing the minimal effective cue at the right time."
visionResearch: "Our research spans streaming perception and memory, task and skill modelling, and interactive assistance. We develop methods that go beyond passive recognition to capture long-horizon dependencies, task graphs, and action scene graphs in egocentric settings, while also adapting across viewpoints and embodiments."
perceptionMemoryDesc: "Streaming observation from egocentric perspectives to maintain a continuous and rich episodic history."
tasksSkillsDesc: "Modeling procedural action structure, assessing execution quality, and detecting fine-grained mistakes."
assistanceInteractionDesc: "Delivering context-aware visual cues and guidance that align with human intent and autonomy."
methodologyValues: "We combine large-scale datasets and benchmarks with controlled user studies and human-in-the-loop protocols to evaluate not only accuracy, but also usefulness, cognitive load, and perceived autonomy. Our projects embrace open science by releasing datasets, benchmarks, and code whenever possible, and emphasize trustworthy, explainable assistance that respects human agency and privacy."
