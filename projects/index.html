Funded Projects

Selected funded projects led or co-led by the group in egocentric vision, procedural understanding, applied memory, and human-centred AI.

Principal Investigator

Egocentric VIsual PROcedural Reasoning for usEr aSsistance (EVIPRORES)

2024–2025 PIACERI Starting Grant, University of Catania

EVIPRORES investigates visual procedural reasoning from egocentric observations, with the goal of enabling AI systems that can interpret task structure and support users during complex activities.

Principal Investigator

Tracking in Egovision for Applied Memory (TEAM)

2023–2025 PRIN 2022 PNRR

TEAM studies tracking-based methods for applied memory in egocentric vision. The project explores how object-level cues in first-person video can support long-term memory representations for AI systems.

Partners: University of Udine and University of Catania
Principal Investigator

EXTRA EYE — Egocentric and eXocenTRic views for object-level human behavior analysis and understanding through tracking in complex spaces

2023–2025 PRIN 2022

EXTRA EYE addresses object-centric human behaviour analysis across egocentric and exocentric views. The project investigates how tracking and cross-view reasoning can improve activity understanding in complex real-world environments.

Partners: University of Macerata, University of Catania, and University of Udine

Procedural Understanding from Egocentric Videos

2024–2025 Toyota

This project focuses on skill level determination from egocentric videos of procedural activities.

Joint PIs: Giovanni Maria Farinella and Antonino Furnari
