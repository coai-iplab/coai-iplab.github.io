Visual Intelligence for
Human-AI Collaboration

We are the Collaborative AI research group, operating within the Image Processing Laboratory (IPLAB) of the University of Catania. We study how AI systems can perceive, understand, and anticipate real‑world procedural activities from first‑person and multi‑view data, to support people with timely, context‑aware feedback and assistance.
